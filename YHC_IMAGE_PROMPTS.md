## How to use this file (3 steps)

1. **Copy a prompt** (the grey box) into ChatGPT. Pick the size written under "Generate at".
2. **Download the image** and rename it to the exact file name given (for example `H01.png`).
3. **Put every file in one folder** — `YHC-assets/generated/` — and tell me. I optimise them, put them
   on the site, and check every page. You can send them in batches (start with ⭐ ones).

**If a prompt says "UPLOAD the real pack":** attach that product photo to your ChatGPT message first
(the files are in `YHC-assets/`, named in the prompt), then paste the prompt. The bottle and label must
stay exactly as they are — if ChatGPT changes the label text, logo or colours, generate again.

**Check every image before sending:** no text or letters in the picture, no extra fingers, no strange
hands, no fake logos, hair looks natural (not shampoo-advert perfect), skin looks real.

---

## Rules (why some images are not here)

These keep the site legal and trustworthy (ASCI, Drugs & Magic Remedies Act, telemedicine rules):

- **No generated Dr. Tyagi, and no generated "doctor" face.** Doctor scenes show hands, a coat, a desk
  or a blurred screen only. Real Dr. Tyagi photos and his video must be **real photos** (shoot list at
  the end).
- **No before/after, no "regrowth" pictures, no people with suspiciously thick hair next to a
  product.** Results must be real patients with written consent — never AI.
- **No testimonials or reviews with faces** — only real, consented ones.
- **No text in images.** The website adds all text.
- **People:** Indian adults, natural everyday hair, real skin. Mostly hands, backs, side views or
  cropped faces — the website's people are "anyone", not patients.
